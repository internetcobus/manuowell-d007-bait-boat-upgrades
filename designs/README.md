# Designs

Printed parts for the upgrade, printed on the K1C (220 × 220 × 250 mm bed). Keep each part's source (OpenSCAD `.scad` or SketchUp `.skp`) and its exported STL together in a folder per part. Export STLs from SketchUp in **millimetres**: it often defaults to inches, which makes the part 25.4 times off.

Parts:

- **[Thruster mounts](thruster-mount/):** version 1 designed 2026-10-08, not yet printed. A pylon on a steel M10 lamp tube through the stock motor-post hole.
- **[Cable holders](cable-holders/):** drawn in SketchUp; STL only.
- **[Servo parts](servo-parts/):** drawn in SketchUp; STL only.
- **[Pixhawk bed](pixhawk-bed/):** drawn in SketchUp; STL only.
- **[GPS antenna spacer](gps-antenna-spacer/):** drawn in SketchUp; STL only.
- **Battery lid:** about 10 mm taller than stock, for the 6S pack's wiring. Bay is about 98 × 73 × 82 mm.
- **PM06 mount:** 35 × 35 mm board, 30.5 × 30.5 mm hole pattern.
